Xenio

Intelligence beyond conversation.

A personal AI assistant and agentic operating system that connects AI to real work on your computer. Give Xenio a goal in plain language: its orchestration engine breaks it into tasks, delegates them to specialised agents, coordinates their work and uses your apps and system tools until it is done.

  • Persistent memory

  • Keyboard & mouse control
